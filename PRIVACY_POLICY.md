## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect
RUNVANTA does not require account registration, login, contacts, microphone, camera access, precise location, or broad photo-library permissions.

## 2. "Play As Anyone" Photo Processing
- RUNVANTA uses the zero-permission Android System Photo Picker (`PickVisualMedia`).
- Selected photographs remain **100% locally on your device** in private app storage.
- Photographs are **never uploaded** to QW HOSTING servers or third-party servers and are **never used for AI model training**.
- Temporary processing files are automatically deleted. You may delete any custom character and its associated face texture at any time inside the app or via **Settings > Reset All Local Progress**.

## 3. Local Persistence
Scores, virtual coins, unlocked worlds, missions, achievements, and settings are stored strictly on-device via Android Room and Jetpack DataStore.

## 4. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)
- RUNVANTA is free to play and supported by Google AdMob (Banner, frequency-capped Interstitial, and optional Rewarded Video ads).
- Where required by law (e.g., EEA/UK/US states), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents a consent dialog allowing personalized or non-personalized ads.
- Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device advertising IDs and diagnostics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

## 5. Children's Privacy
RUNVANTA does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe any issue exists, contact `info@qwhosting.com`.
